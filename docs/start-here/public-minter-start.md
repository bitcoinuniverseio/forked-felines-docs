# Public minter start

**Goal**: mint a Forked Feline at the public rate, with no prior connection to KNOT HEADS.

## What it costs

| Component | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Collection price | 8,888 sats |
| Bitcoin network fee | live, depends on the fee rate you choose |
| Inscription delivery | live, shown on the quote |
| Service fee | 1,500 sats |

The site sums these into **one exact total** on a signed quote before your wallet opens. There are no hidden costs, no percentage fees, and nothing added after you approve. If your payment later needs a fee bump to confirm, each accepted bump adds exactly 1,500 sats of service fee and is shown to you first; a rejected or failed bump adds nothing.

## Steps

1. Open [forkedfelines.art/mint](https://forkedfelines.art/mint). Read the posted rates and the live supply.
2. Enter the Bitcoin address that should receive the Feline. Any address you control works; a Taproot address from an Ordinals-aware wallet is easiest to manage afterwards.
3. Pick a quantity and a fee rate, read the itemized quote, and choose Pay with Wallet.
4. Approve the payment in your wallet if it matches the printed total, then watch the kitchen ticket until your Feline is SERVED.

## What to check before you approve

- The amount in the wallet equals the quoted total, to the sat.
- The destination address in the wallet equals the payment address printed on the bill.
- You were not asked to sign anything other than that one payment.

If any of those differ, reject the request and read [What we will never ask](../safety/what-we-will-never-ask.md).

## Which Feline do you get?

The next available edition, drawn from the frozen recipe. Every Feline was generated from a seed frozen before the first mint, so nothing about your timing or wallet influences the art. Odds for every trait are published; see [Traits and odds](../collection/traits-and-odds.md). Unreleased artwork stays private until it is confirmed on Bitcoin.
