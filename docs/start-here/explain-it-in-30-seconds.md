# Explain it in 30 seconds

Use this when someone asks what you just minted.

## One sentence

> Forked Felines is 3,333 cats drawn once from a frozen recipe, inscribed whole on Bitcoin, and every one of them can show its working.

## Thirty seconds

> It is the official companion collection to KNOT HEADS. Each Feline is a hand-drawn SVG generated deterministically from a frozen seed, and the entire artwork is inscribed on Bitcoin itself, not a link to an image somewhere. Before any Feline is even reserved, the project records the exact bytes it will inscribe, their digest, and the odds of every trait, and publishes that evidence on the Feline's own page. Knot Heads holders from the block 963,238 snapshot mint free; everyone else pays 8,888 sats plus the usual Bitcoin costs, all shown as one exact total before the wallet opens.

## The three facts people ask about

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How many? | 3,333 maximum. The site counts real confirmed inscriptions. |
| What does it cost? | 8,888 sats collection price, or 0 sats with a Knot Heads credit. Network, delivery, and a 1,500-sat service fee are itemized separately. |
| Where is the art? | On Bitcoin, whole. The SVG bytes are the inscription. |
