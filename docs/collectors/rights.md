# Rights

What owning a Forked Felines inscription means, stated exactly, versioned and hashed at [/rights](https://forkedfelines.art/rights).

## The short version

- The inscription is yours: the record itself is associated with the Bitcoin output you control.
- The artwork's copyright is not transferred to you.
- Personal, non-commercial display is granted.
- Commercial use: **not granted**. Derivatives: **not granted**. Trademark use: **not granted**.

Where a right is absent, the policy says "not granted" outright. Nothing is implied, and no surface may dress an absence up as permission.

## Versioned, hashed, and history you can check

Every policy version is a hashed document. Version history is public, each version names what it supersedes, and the current document downloads immutably from `/api/v1/rights/policy/download`. A file you already hold stays governed by the version recorded in its receipt, even after a new version or a transfer.

## Holder media

Holders who prove current ownership through the portfolio proof flow can generate media packs: square avatar, banner crop, phone wallpaper, provenance card, and proof QR. Packs are limited to approved derivatives, carry a rights receipt naming the policy version, and the download authorization expires. None of the private source layers, unrevealed traits, or generator inputs are ever exposed.
