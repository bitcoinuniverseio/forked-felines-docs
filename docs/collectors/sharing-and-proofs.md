# Sharing and proofs

Every confirmed Feline carries a full set of portable, verifiable evidence.

## On every item page

| Feature | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Native share | The browser's share sheet where one exists; a clean copy of the permanent link everywhere else. No tracking parameters. |
| Copy inscription id | Pulled from the permanent record at press time, never a stale copy. |
| Proof bundle | One deterministic JSON file: the inclusion proof, the authoritative item record, the collection root, the seal when confirmed, the artwork, recipe, and manifest hashes, and verifier instructions. |
| Provenance QR | Resolves to the permanent item verification page and carries the edition, the proof root, and the seal identifier. Never a raw proof, a secret, or an address. |

The proof bundle digest is shown on the item page, so the file you share today can be checked against what the page served.

## Social cards

Item cards are deterministic: the exact edition, approved artwork, a short trait summary, and the verified inscription identifier. A verified listing price appears only while the listing is current, and the card drops the price the moment the listing is sold, withdrawn, expired, or stale. Owner identity never appears unless a holder deliberately makes it public.
