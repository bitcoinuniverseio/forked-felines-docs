# My Booth

My Booth is your table at the house: every Feline delivered to an address, every active order, credits, standing, and the door to support, in one place.

## Looking up a booth

Open [forkedfelines.art/my-booth](https://forkedfelines.art/my-booth) and enter a recipient address, or connect a wallet to fill the field. The booth shows:

- **Your Felines**: every confirmed Feline delivered to that address, with portraits, traits, and links to each one's page on the wall.
- **Newly arrived**: what confirmed since you last looked, using only your own browser's memory of your last visit.
- **Active orders**: live kitchen tickets for anything in flight.
- **Order history**: settled, expired, and refunded orders.
- **Knot Heads standing**: verified head count and remaining free-mint credits for that address.
- **Remediation**: if the address qualifies, the remediation choice and its status. See [Remediation and refunds](remediation-and-refunds.md).

## Pairing cards

A Feline delivered to an address that also holds Knot Heads can be shown side by side with its Knot Head on a pairing card, a shareable image drawn from real ownership. Nothing on the card claims value; it records the pairing and the editions.

## Privacy, plainly

- Lookups are by address. The house does not know, and does not try to guess, which addresses belong to the same person.
- Booth lookups do not create a public profile. Detailed holdings are not exposed through any unauthenticated profiling endpoint.
- "Newly arrived" state lives in your browser's storage only. Nothing about your visits leaves your device.
- Connecting a wallet to fill the field discloses nothing beyond what the wallet itself shares with the page.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| My Feline is not in the booth | Check the recipient address on your order's quote; the Feline is at that exact address. If the order is not SERVED yet, it is still in flight on the kitchen ticket. |
| Can someone else see my booth? | Anyone can look up any address; that is the nature of a public chain. The booth adds no private information to what Bitcoin already publishes. |
| Multiple addresses? | Look them up one at a time. The house deliberately does not link addresses to each other. |
