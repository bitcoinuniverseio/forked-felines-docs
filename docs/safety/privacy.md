# Privacy

What the house stores, what it deliberately does not, and what lives only in your own browser.

## What is stored

- **Orders.** A mint order records its recipient address, payment details, quote, and state history. This is the minimum required to deliver a Feline and to prove, later, exactly what happened.
- **Support conversations.** What you write to support, so the conversation can continue.
- **Remediation elections.** Append-only records of each qualifying Feline's choice, bound to the addresses involved.

## What is deliberately not stored

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to register, so there is no email, password, or profile to hold.
- **No identity linkage.** The house does not infer that two addresses belong to one person, and does not build cross-address profiles. Booth lookups are per address, by design.
- **No marketing analytics on wallets.** Wallet addresses are not fed into analytics or advertising tooling.
- **No behavioral tracking.** No third-party trackers, no ad pixels, no session replay.

## What lives only in your browser

- **"Newly arrived" state.** The wall and your booth remember your last visit in your browser's own storage to show what is new. Nothing about your visits leaves your device, and a browser that refuses storage still works; it just cannot say what is new.
- **Sound preference.** Off by default; your choice is remembered locally.

## What the chain publishes anyway

Bitcoin is public. Inscriptions, addresses, and payments are visible to everyone forever, on every chain explorer. The house adds no private information on top of that public record, but it cannot remove what the chain itself shows. Choose receiving addresses with that in mind.

## Documentation site

This documentation site serves static pages with no analytics, no cookies, and no wallet connection of any kind.
