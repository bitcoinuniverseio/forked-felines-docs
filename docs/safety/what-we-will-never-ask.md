# What we will never ask

Print this page in your head. If Forked Felines seems to be asking for any item on it, you are not talking to Forked Felines.

## Never, under any circumstances

- Your **seed phrase**, recovery words, private key, or wallet file. Not for verification, not for support, not for a refund, not once.
- A payment to any address **not printed on a signed quote** in the app.
- A payment to "unlock", "validate", "sync", "migrate", or "reactivate" anything.
- A signature on a **blank, hex-only, or unexplained message**.
- A token approval or delegation of any kind.
- Software downloads, browser extensions, or "mint tools".
- Your email, phone number, or identity documents to mint or hold.
- A DM conversation. Staff will never DM first, on any platform, ever.

## What the house does ask, exhaustively

| Request | When | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Bitcoin address in the recipient field | Minting, booth lookup | Says where the Feline goes or which booth to show |
| Approval of one payment for one printed total | After you read the signed quote | Pays that bill, once |
| One labelled BIP-322 message naming forkedfelines.art | Member verification, remediation elections | Proves address control to this site; moves nothing |

That is the complete list. It will stay this short.

## If you already gave something away

- **Seed phrase**: assume total compromise. Move remaining assets from a different, clean device to a brand-new wallet immediately. Inscriptions first if fees allow, then coins.
- **A signature or payment you now doubt**: open [Getting support](../help/getting-support.md) from the real site and describe it. Support can read your order's true state and tell you what actually happened on chain.

## Reporting impersonators

Report phishing sites and fake accounts through [reporting a security problem](reporting-a-security-problem.md). Screenshots and URLs help the next collector.
