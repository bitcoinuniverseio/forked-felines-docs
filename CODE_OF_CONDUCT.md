# Code of conduct

This is a small documentation repository for a collectible with questionable table manners. The people in it behave better than the cats.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep discussions about the documentation and the product facts.
- Protect other people's privacy: no posting of other people's addresses, orders, or holdings beyond what a report genuinely needs.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Scams, phishing links, fake support, or impersonation of the project or its staff.
- Financial advice, price talk presented as fact, or pump content. This project promises no value and neither do its threads.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content and restrict participation to keep the space useful and safe. Impersonation and scam content is removed on sight and reported to GitHub.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems through the in-app [support desk](https://forkedfelines.art/support) or by contacting the maintainers via this repository. Security problems follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
